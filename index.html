Welcome to the Citizen Assistance and Benefits Portal

The AuraCare Citizen Assistance and Benefits Portal is a centralized online platform developed by the fictional National Social Welfare and Citizen Protection Authority. Designed to streamline public access to social protection resources, this portal enables citizens to explore financial assistance programs, check eligibility requirements, and submit claims entirely digitally. By leveraging web technologies, AuraCare aims to bridge the gap between national welfare initiatives and the communities that need them most.

Through this portal, citizens can learn about various benefits tailored to different lifespans and socioeconomic needs, from retirement pension grants to disaster emergency relief funds. The portal is open to all qualified Filipino citizens, including senior citizens, low-income families, displaced workers, and individuals with disabilities. By registering and submitting applications online, beneficiaries can track their requests transparently without visiting physical government offices.

Featured Assistance Services

We provide multiple financial support and social safety net programs to help citizens overcome economic challenges. Discover our key programs below:

Employment

Income Assistance

Provides temporary financial support and monthly wage subsidies for low-income earners, underemployed individuals, and involuntarily displaced formal workers.

Basic Qualifications: Involuntary separation or verified monthly household income below the national poverty line.

Retirement Assistance

Offers comprehensive monthly pension payouts, medical support grants, and social welfare programs to ensure dignified living for senior citizens.

Family Welfare

Family Assistance

Targeted conditional cash transfers, nutritional allowances, educational stipends, and welfare programs designed to support low-income families and solo parents.

Emergency Assistance

Provides immediate emergency cash grants, crisis relief services, and medical assistance for families displaced by typhoons, floods, fires, or epidemics.

Important Reminders

Please review these guidelines carefully before proceeding with any claims or benefit submissions:

  • Keep your profile updated: Ensure that all your personal information, contact number, email, and current home address are accurate and match your official government IDs.
  • Prepare your documents: Before starting an application, scan and save clear digital copies of your required documents (e.g., Valid ID, Barangay Residency Certificate, Proof of Eligibility).
  • Check eligibility details: Carefully read the qualifications for each assistance program on the Benefits page to ensure you meet all requirements before filing.
  • Save confirmation details: Keep a record of your Application Reference Number and download a copy of your submitted application form for tracking purposes.
  • Monitor application status: Do not submit multiple applications for the same benefit. Use the application tracking tool on the Claims page to check status updates.
  • Review eligibility requirements: Carefully read the specific qualifications for each program on our Benefits page to ensure successful approval.
  • Keep copies of submitted documents: Always keep physical or digital backups of all paperwork and submitted credentials for your personal reference.
  • Check the status of your application: Use your tracking code regularly on the Claims status portal to receive real-time processing updates.
