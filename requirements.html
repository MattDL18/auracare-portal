Primary Identification Requirements

Every claim requires at least one primary government-issued ID card. The ID must be valid, contain a clear photograph, and bear the applicant's signature:

  • Philippine National Identification Card (PhilID / ePhilID): The official foundational ID of the Republic of the Philippines. Recommended for fast-track authentication.
  • Philippine Passport: Issued by the Department of Foreign Affairs. Must be within its validity date.
  • Social Security System (SSS) / GSIS UMID Card: Unified Multi-Purpose Identification Card showing member numbers.
  • Senior Citizen ID Card: Issued by the Office of Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA). Mandatory for Senior Pension claims.
  • Persons with Disabilities (PWD) ID Card: Issued by the National Council on Disability Affairs (NCDA). Required for PWD support claims.

Proof of Income and Socioeconomic Status

To qualify for low-income allowances or family sustenance subsidies, applicants must demonstrate financial eligibility through one of the following:

  • Certificate of Indigency: Issued by your local Barangay Chairman stating that the household falls under the poverty line.
  • Annual Income Tax Return (ITR): Form 1701/1700 for the preceding calendar year, showing total taxable income.
  • Certificate of Low Income: Issued by the local Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO).
  • Payslips / Certificate of Employment: For formal workers, matching the last three (3) months of compensation.

Barangay Clearance and Residency Guidelines

Every applicant must prove active localized physical residency to ensure local government accountability. Follow these guidelines:

  1. Visit your local Barangay Hall: Request a Barangay Residency Certificate or Barangay Clearance for social welfare assistance.
  2. Present local proof of address: Show a utility bill, voter registration, or rent contract verifying your address.
  3. Verify the validity date: Certificates must have been issued within the last ninety (90) calendar days.
  4. Verify signature and seal: Ensure the document is signed by the Barangay Captain and contains the official dry seal.
