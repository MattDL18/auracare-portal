How to Apply for Fictional Benefits

To file a benefit claim successfully, please follow our step-by-step application workflow:

  1. Determine the appropriate benefit: Choose the assistance program on our Benefits page that fits your current socioeconomic or life situation.
  2. Review the eligibility requirements: Read the qualifications list thoroughly to ensure you are fully eligible for the program.
  3. Prepare the required documents: Scan and compile all necessary IDs, certifications, and banking details into clean digital files.
  4. Complete the online application: Scroll down to our online application form below and enter your details truthfully.
  5. Submit the application: Press the Submit button to forward your application. Write down the generated Reference Number.
  6. Wait for confirmation: Our verification team will review your submitted credentials and notify you via email or SMS.
  7. Check the application status: Use our status lookup tool below at any time to monitor the progress of your application.

Fictional Claim Types

AuraCare categorizes applications into four distinct claim classes. Each program has unique conditions:

Retirement

Retirement Claim

Filed by senior citizens or their authorized representatives to register for standard monthly retirement pensions and healthcare support.

Family Welfare

Family Assistance Claim

Submitted by indigent households and solo parents to request quarterly conditional subsidies, nutrition allowances, and educational stipends.

Calamity Response

Emergency Assistance Claim

Filed by households residing in officially declared disaster areas to request rapid cash assistance, calamity grants, or displaced shelters.

PWD Support

Disability Assistance Claim

Submitted by certified Persons with Disabilities (PWD) or their caregivers to register for monthly living and mobility support grants.
