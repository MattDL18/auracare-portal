Benefit Categories

AuraCare offers at least five core fictional benefit programs targeting specific demographic groups. Browse the cards below to see the details of each program:

Retirement

Senior Pension Grant

A monthly financial grant designed to support indigent senior citizens and retired workers, ensuring access to nutrition and basic healthcare services.

Who May Qualify: Filipino citizens aged 60 or older with verified low-income status.

Basic Requirements: Senior Citizen ID, Birth Certificate, and Barangay Residency Certificate.

Family Welfare

Family Sustenance Subsidy

Conditional quarterly grants helping low-income households with school-age children cover the costs of basic educational needs and health monitoring.

Who May Qualify: Registered indigent families with children enrolled in elementary or high school.

Basic Requirements: Certificate of Indigency, School Enrollment Records, and Parent Valid ID.
Apply Now →
Displaced Workers

Transitional Wage Stipend

Bi-monthly wage support for workers affected by company closures or downsizing, provided they are enrolled in certified technical upskilling courses.

Who May Qualify: Involuntarily displaced employees actively seeking re-employment.

Basic Requirements: Termination Letter, Training Enrollment Proof, and SSS/GSIS separation cert.
Apply Now →
PWD Support

Mobility Support Allowance

Monthly medical and living subsidies tailored for persons with physical, sensory, or cognitive disabilities to offset therapeutic and mobility-aid expenses.

Who May Qualify: Registered Persons with Disabilities (PWD) who are unable to engage in regular employment.

Basic Requirements: Valid PWD ID, Government Physician Medical Certificate, and proof of address.
Apply Now →
Disaster Relief

Calamity Emergency Relief

A one-time emergency relief grant assisting citizens and families residing in localized zones declared under an official State of Calamity.

Who May Qualify: Homeowners or renters with verified property damage or livelihood loss due to natural disasters.

Basic Requirements: Barangay Damage Assessment Form, Calamity ID Card, and any primary government ID.

General Eligibility Considerations

To qualify for any benefit program through the AuraCare portal, applicants must meet the following structural criteria. Fictional eligibility guidelines are outlined below:

  • Age Criteria: Specific age floors apply (e.g., retirement pension requires a minimum age of 60 years, while transitional employment stipends apply strictly to citizens of working age, 18-65).
  • Household Income Threshold: Household earnings must fall below national poverty line markers. Indigent and low-income status is verified through municipal database reviews.
  • Active Contribution/Registration: Member registration must be active. Programs may require a minimum number of prior monthly contributions or verified community service points.
  • Local Residency: Applicants must be certified residents of their respective municipality or barangay for at least six (6) consecutive months.
  • Authentic Documentation: All submitted documentation must be original or certified copy, and free of inconsistencies or verification issues to prevent application delays.

General Documentary Requirements

Applicants are requested to compile the following five primary items before starting the online filing process:

  1. Valid Government-Issued ID: A clear photo of a primary identification card (e.g., PhilID, Passport, Senior Citizen ID, PWD Card, UMID, Driver's License).
  2. Official Application Form: The fully filled out claim sheet, downloadable from this portal or completed directly online.
  3. Proof of Program Eligibility: Document verifying your status (e.g., Physician's Certificate for disability, Termination Notice for wage support, Indigency Cert for family aid).
  4. Supporting Community Clearances: A Barangay Residency Certificate or local social worker assessment clearance proving active physical residency in the area.
  5. Valid Payment Account Proof: Verification of your receiving channel, such as bank account details, e-wallet registration screenshot, or cash card details.
